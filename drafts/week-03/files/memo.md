# Memo: November results and the December plan

**To:** Managing Director
**From:** Commercial analytics
**Date:** 3 December 2025
**Re:** November trading and stock recommendation for December

## Summary

November was our strongest month on record. Revenue reached £1.41M, up 43% on October, on 2,579 invoices from 1,623 customers. Growth has been continuous since August. We recommend increasing December stock by 30% across the gift ranges and budgeting 2026 on 15% month-on-month growth.

## Monthly revenue

| Month | Revenue | Invoices |
|---|---|---|
| August | £0.75M | 1,379 |
| September | £0.98M | 1,661 |
| October | £1.20M | 2,136 |
| November | £1.41M | 2,579 |

Figures are net of cancellations and zero-price lines.

## Markets

The United Kingdom accounted for 88% of November revenue. The next four markets (Ireland, France, the Netherlands, Germany) contributed about 2% each. No market outside the UK grew faster than the UK in November.

## Products

The best-selling product by revenue in November was the Rabbit Night Light (£29k), followed by the Paper Chain Kit 50's Christmas (£27k). Christmas lines dominate the top twenty. Demand for small gift items is growing across the sector: 62% of UK shoppers now buy stocking fillers online, according to the Institute of Gift Retailing's *Festive Outlook 2025*.

## Recommendation

1. Increase December stock of the top twenty November products by 30%.
2. Extend the online promotion on the Rabbit Night Light through 20 December.
3. Set the 2026 budget at 15% month-on-month revenue growth, in line with the trend since August.

Prepared with the November export of the sales system.
